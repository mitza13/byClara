2023

2024

28/01/2024 - I draw this woman quite a few times, and I did a small series of drawings inspired by tarot cards. I drew her as Lady Justice. This one was more blended out, and another experiment on art style. This was the first time I tried out blending modes besides the simple ones, and was pleasantly surprised. I still, however, don’t have the hands quite down at this point in time because I made her right arm black because her hand looked silly
9/3/2024 - when on vacation with my younger sister a photo was taken of her and I used it as inspiration for my next drawing. I like the way the sweater and lighting was done, but I still cannot draw hands because she has 6 fingers in one hand. I was still developing my style, trying to lean more towards semi-realism than cartoon but I couldn’t quite get the face to work out for me either
17/3/2024 - From one of my favourite books I decided to draw the main character in a beautiful dress. I like how she turned out, and the fabric of the dress looks very nice, but there are still inconsistencies in style. It is, however, getting better
31/7/2024 - I drew that woman again, except this time in a completely different style. I did say I was experimenting with styles
12/8/2024 - I drew reference from a drawing off of Pinterest, and with much difficulty I produced this. The semi-realistic face is exactly what I wanted, but this took so long that I knew it would be difficult to reproduce. I only needed to practice
26/10/2024 - The recurring woman, again, also in another style. I like the minimalist of this one, but her face proportions don’t really work out as her nose looks odd on her face. I was still trying out new styles and colour palettes

2025

25/5/2025 - I went to a museum in Buchareşt, and fell in love with one of the paintings. I decided to recreate something similar and ended up with this
10/6/2025 - a very fantastical take on a siren. I wanted to show a terrifying powerful creature, coming out of the water as if she were water. I tried another style, one with a messy sketch but a very precise rendering of the shadows and colours
11/6/2025 - I decided to draw my sister again, but I didn’t want to draw her in the same way as before. Experimenting with another style, I drew her all in her favourite colour of the time. She loved it more than the last one
21/7/2025 - I played around a little with graphic design and this came out. I quite like the way the real stoplight distracts you from the fact the background is completely hand drawn
8/8/2025 - a very simple landscape based on a landscape my friend took a photo of. I decided to add on to the photo, adding in shadows and light, and blurring things here and there. Not quite a painting, and more editing an already existing photo
22/8/2025 - I decided to stop drawing other people’s main characters and to do my own. The style is again very different, but still similar to things I have done before. I did t refine the sketch, and instead used it as the line art, while I rendered her face really well. I wanted her face to be the focus, so I spent no attention to the clothes. I know that warmth is associated with things that are kind and happy, so I wanted this woman who is strange looking and very much mean spirited to be completely portrayed in warm browns and reds. No hands are in this drawing, and that is on purpose, but you can tell I improved in drawing noses. The symbolism with the wolf also ties it more into being my character rather than some random drawing.
25/8/2025 - Compared to the previous drawing, this is the completely opposite. It’s a very serene drawing, and the blues reflect that. I was leaning again towards the siren fantasy creature, but I wanted it almost to be like a creature in disguise, one that had to hold appearances up all day long, but here in the depths of the lake, she could show her true self and relax
12/10/2025 - this was a reference to a beautiful photo on Pinterest of a porcelain statue looking defeated as cracks appeared in her face. The photograph is a metaphor how women are o lot supposed to look pretty and not speak opinions, quiet and eautoful like a statue. I took inspiration, but instead of making her look defeated at the ground, I made her look right at the viewer. I wanted to show though the expectations of her are only to be pretty and quiet, and though her mouth has been broken into with a hammer, she is still defiant as she challenges the viewer
19/10/2025 - I tried another landscape drawing in an effort to be aesthetic. I like how the colour palette turned out, but it was more of a mindless sketch rather than a proper painting with meaning and thought
26/10/2025 - I was forced to watch a horrible movie with horrible costume design, so I decided to draw my own. I did get a little off track as I drew the daughter of the Queen of Hearts, but I am happy how it turned out
